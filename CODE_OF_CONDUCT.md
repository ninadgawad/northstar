# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Northstar to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile at <https://github.com/ninadgawad>. Reports are handled confidentially.
